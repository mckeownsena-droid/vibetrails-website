Website terms.

Basic terms for using this website and submitting an adventure enquiry.

Last updated: August 2026.

Booking enquiries

Submitting an enquiry through this website does not by itself create or confirm a booking. A booking is confirmed only after VibeTrails communicates availability, price, payment requirements and any trip-specific conditions to you.

Trip information

Dates, meeting points, inclusions, prices and itineraries can differ by adventure. Guests should rely on the final trip information sent directly by VibeTrails for the specific experience they are joining.

Outdoor activities

Hiking, waterfall visits, camping, road travel and other outdoor experiences can involve physical activity, changing weather and natural terrain. Guests should follow safety guidance, disclose relevant practical concerns when appropriate and make reasonable decisions about their ability to participate.

Changes

Outdoor itineraries may need to change because of weather, access, safety, transport or other circumstances outside reasonable control. VibeTrails will communicate material changes to confirmed guests when necessary.

Contact

Questions about a specific booking or trip should be directed to 059 313 2204 or 054 838 9615.
