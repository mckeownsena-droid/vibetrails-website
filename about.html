Meet VibeTrails.

VibeTrails is a Ghanaian event and adventure hub creating memorable group experiences across Ghana. We bring people together through road trips, hiking, waterfalls, beaches, camping and other outdoor adventures.

What VibeTrails does

VibeTrails helps people discover Ghana through shared experiences. We organize and promote group adventures where the destination is only part of the story. The experience also includes the journey, the people, the activities and the memories created along the way.

Group adventures

Planned experiences for people who want to explore Ghana with a community of fellow travellers and adventurers.

Road trips

Trips that turn the journey itself into part of the experience, with shared moments along the way.

Nature & hiking

Waterfalls, mountains, trails and hidden gems for people who want to experience Ghana beyond the ordinary.

Beach & camping

Coastal escapes, camping experiences, games and relaxed group moments by the water.

Where we go

VibeTrails experiences have included destinations and trails such as Wli Falls, Mount Afadjato, Amedzofe and Mount Gemi, Adom Waterfalls, Shai Hills and Kakum. New adventures are announced as they open.

Our purpose

We believe adventure feels better when people experience it together. VibeTrails exists to help people explore Ghana, connect with others and create stories worth remembering.
