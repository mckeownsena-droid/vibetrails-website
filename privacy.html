Privacy.

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Last updated: August 2026.

VibeTrails respects the privacy of people who contact us. This website is primarily an information and enquiry site.

Information you provide

If you use the booking enquiry form, the details you enter are used to prepare a WhatsApp message. The website itself does not store those form entries in a database. Once you send the message through WhatsApp, the information is handled through that service and by VibeTrails for responding to your enquiry and coordinating your trip.

Contact details

Phone or WhatsApp details may be used to answer questions, confirm bookings, share trip preparation information and communicate changes relating to an adventure you are joining.

Photography

VibeTrails experiences may involve photography or video. Where media is used for promotional purposes, guests should raise any concerns with the VibeTrails team.

Third-party services

This site links to WhatsApp and TikTok. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.
