The official VibeTrails website

VibeTrails is a Ghanaian event & adventure hub.

VibeTrails creates experiences that help people explore Ghana, connect with others and make memorable stories. Our experiences include road trips, hiking, waterfalls, beaches, camping, outdoor adventures and group events.

What VibeTrails is known for

VibeTrails brings people together around real destinations and shared experiences across Ghana. We promote and organize adventures such as waterfall trips, mountain hikes, road trips, beach escapes, camping experiences and community activities.

Where VibeTrails operates

VibeTrails is focused on Ghana and Ghanaian destinations. Experiences can include places such as Wli Falls, Mount Afadjato, Amedzofe, Adom Waterfalls, Shai Hills and Kakum, with new destinations and events announced over time.

Official channels

This website, vibetrails.org, is the official online home of VibeTrails. Follow @vibe_trails on TikTok for current announcements and upcoming experiences.
